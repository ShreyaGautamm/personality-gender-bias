Personality Shapes Gender Bias in Persona-Conditioned LLM Narratives Across English and Hindi

An empirical investigation across 23,400 generated stories from six state-of-the-art LLMs, demonstrating that HEXACO and Dark Triad personality traits act as systematic amplifiers and attenuators of gender stereotypes in occupational contexts.

Measurement Methodology

The Centroid-Based Sentence Embedding Metric

Measuring gender stereotyping at the sentence level in a shared multilingual semantic space.

Step 1
Stereotype Lexicons
Curated parallel word lists in English and Hindi (~200 items each covering male and female traits, occupations, and actions), verified with native speakers.
Step 2
Template Contextualization
Words are transformed into contextualized sentences using grammatical templates (e.g., "This is [X]" and "वह [X] है") to preserve POS semantics.
Step 3
IndicSBERT Centroids
Embedded into an Indic-tailored sentence space to compute the male reference centroid cmale and female centroid cfemale.
Sentence-Level Difference of Cosines
bias(si) = cos(si, cmale) − cos(si, cfemale)
Aggregated to story level via peak salience: story(S) = bias(sk) where k = argmax |bias(si)|

Empirical Results

Personality as a Systematic Bias Modulator

Dark Triad traits act as powerful male-bias amplifiers, while prosocial traits exhibit heterogeneous effects across 6 language models.

OLS Personality Trait Effects on Bias Score
Positive values indicate shift toward male-stereotypical language (p < 0.05)
← Female Attenuation (-0.02) Neutral (0.00) Male Amplification (+0.02) →
Stereotypic Leaning Across Model Architectures
Comparing Baseline (without personality) vs. Persona-conditioned prompts
RQ1: Amplification vs Attenuation
Dark Triad Consistently Amplifies Bias

Machiavellianism (+0.021) and Psychopathy (+0.018) consistently produce the largest positive shifts in stereotypic language across all 6 models.

RQ2: Trait vs Gender Relative Power
Personality Often Trumps Gender Labels

Coefficients for Dark Triad traits exceed the coefficient of explicit gender assignment (+0.011 for male), showing personality cues dominate narrative framing.

RQ3: Hindi vs English Dynamics
Morphology Shifts Personality Variance

Hindi exhibits higher baseline male skew due to cultural defaults and verb morphology, but English displays higher personality-driven modulation range.
